Job-change detection

Spot job changes across 10,000 HubSpot leads on a regular cycle.

Stack
n8n · Apify · HubSpot (custom contact properties)
Decisions
  • n8n orchestrates the run and Apify fetches the profiles.
  • Each lead's previous state is saved as a snapshot in custom HubSpot contact properties, and every sweep compares against it.
  • Weekly sweeps of 2,500 records, so four sweeps cover all 10,000 leads roughly once a month.
Results
10,000HubSpot leads in scope
2,500records per weekly sweep
Est. impact
Estimate, not measured
~2,000job changes surfaced per year across 10,000 leads, about 170 a month
2xtypical win rate on deals with a former champion (39% vs under 19% SaaS average)

Basis: assumes about 20% of contacts change jobs each year, in line with UserGems (20%+ of CRM contacts have moved) and SignalHire (30% of B2B records go stale a year). Win rates from Champify.

Case study · Comcap, M&A

M&A buyer and seller prospecting in Clay

Find buy-side and sell-side target companies from signals, reach the right decision makers, and keep HubSpot current without adding records twice.

Stack
Clay (find companies, signals, find people, AI columns) · HubSpot
Decisions
  • Separate Clay tables for the buyer side and the seller side, each identifying companies from signals.
  • Every target company is checked against HubSpot first. If the company is already there, its record is updated instead of being added again.
  • Up to two senior decision makers per company, found in Clay. AI columns split each result into full name, first name, last name and job title.
  • Email and LinkedIn are looked up by name and company, and QA tables check the output before it goes to HubSpot.
  • Run conditions skip rows that don't meet the criteria, so lookups only run where they are needed.
Results
2senior decision makers targeted per company
93% to 96%of companies with senior contacts found, on the tables shown below
Est. impact
Estimate, not measured
up to ~190senior contacts per 100 target companies (2 per company at 93% to 96% coverage)
~30%of existing HubSpot contacts likely refreshed a year by updating instead of re-adding

Basis: coverage from the Clay tables shown above. Annual record decay of about 30% from SignalHire.

Prospect list and qualification engine

Source and qualify a prospect list of 185,000+ leads.

Stack
Apollo · Prospeo · Apify · Claude · ZeroBounce · CRM
Decisions
  • Apollo, Prospeo and Apify are combined for extraction.
  • Claude qualifies each lead against ICP criteria.
  • ZeroBounce verifies emails before leads go out.
  • Qualified leads are distributed through the CRM.
Results
185,000+leads sourced and qualified
Est. impact
Estimate, not measured
3,700 to 9,250bounces avoided if the 185,000+ list were emailed once without verification
under 2%target bounce rate after verification, the safe sending limit

Basis: unverified lists bounce at 2% to 5%+ and verified lists at 0.5% to 1.5%, per Prospeo. Assumes every lead is emailed once.
